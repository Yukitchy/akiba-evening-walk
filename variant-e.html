Three routes
one night

Not the shopping tour. Three five-hour walks through the history of Akihabara and old Tokyo — one stays deep in Akihabara, one heads to the markets, one to the book town. Pick yours.

Pick your route

Route AA

Akihabara in Depth

One district, read deeply

Kanda Myojin at dusk → back streets → under the tracks → parts alleys → Chuo-dori neon → Radio Kaikan → dinner

  • The shrine on the hill — Kanda Myojin, founded 730, moved here in 1616 to guard Edo Castle.
  • Where Electric Town was born — postwar radio stalls, rehoused under the arches in 1949; the maze still trades.
  • Neon at its peak — appliances, computers, anime: one street, three eras.

About 3 km · finish Akihabara. Best for: first-timers, and anime/game fans who want the story, not the shopping.

Route BB

Akiba → Okachimachi → Ameyoko

Electric Town to market town

Akihabara → Suehirocho → 2k540 artisan arches → Okachimachi → Ameyoko market → izakaya lanes

  • Ameyoko, while it is awake — postwar black market turned market street: “Candy Store Alley” meets “America Alley”. Shops close from about 19:00–20:00, so the market comes first.
  • 2k540 — artisan studios in the arches, named for the marker 2 km 540 m from Tokyo Station.
  • The lanes light up — dinner and a drink under the tracks as the market sleeps.

About 4 km · finish Okachimachi / Ueno. Best for: food-and-market people and repeat Tokyo visitors.

Route CC

Akiba → Kanda → Jimbocho

Old Tokyo, page by page

Kanda Myojin → Manseibashi & mAAch → Kanda station quarter → under-track lanes → Jimbocho → the quiet book town

  • The vanished station — Manseibashi, 1912, by Tatsuno Kingo; closed 1943, reborn as mAAch ecute. Coffee in the arches.
  • Old Kanda — one of Edo’s merchant and craftsman quarters.
  • Jimbocho, open and closed — around 130 secondhand bookshops; browse first, then the shuttered, lamplit street.

About 4 km · finish Jimbocho. Best for: book lovers, history lovers, and anyone who prefers calm to neon.

  • January evenings are cold — bring a warm layer; we are outside for much of the walk.
  • Closing times shape the routes: parts shops and market stalls wind down around 19:00–20:00; big stores, arcades and restaurants stay open late.
  • Bring some cash — small stalls, market shops and shrine charms are often cash only.
  • Shrine steps: routes A and C include one flight of steps at Kanda Myojin.
