Tour proposalAkihabara, Tokyo

One evening, three routes

None of these is the shopping tour. All three start in Akihabara at 16:00, run about five hours, and walk the history and culture of the town into the night. They differ in where the story goes: deeper into Akihabara, north to the market streets, or west into old Kanda and the book town. Pick the one that fits you.

Start16:00, Akihabara Station
DurationAbout 5 hours
WalkingAbout 3–4 km
GroupPrivate
ROUTE A

Akihabara in Depth

One district, read deeply — how a black market became Electric Town, then the otaku capital.

FlowKanda Myojin at dusk → back streets → under the tracks → parts alleys → Chuo-dori neon → Radio Kaikan → night streets and dinner
DistanceAbout 3 km · finish in Akihabara
HighlightsKanda Myojin — founded in 730, moved to this hill in 1616 to guard Edo Castle; it still watches over the neighbourhood. The 1949 maze — postwar radio-parts stalls rehoused under the arches, still trading. Chuo-dori at peak neon — appliances, computers, then anime: one street, three eras.
Best forA first evening in Akihabara; anime and game fans who want the story behind the shops.
ROUTE B

Akiba → Okachimachi → Ameyoko

Electric Town to market town — how working Tokyo eats, drinks and trades after dark.

FlowAkihabara → Suehirocho back streets → 2k540 artisan arches → Okachimachi → Ameyoko market → under-track izakaya lanes
DistanceAbout 4 km · finish on the Okachimachi–Ueno side
HighlightsAmeyoko — a postwar black market turned market street: candy stalls made it “Candy Store Alley”, American-goods shops added “America Alley”. Shops close from about 19:00–20:00, so the market comes first, honestly. 2k540 — artisan studios in the arches, named for the railway marker 2 km 540 m from Tokyo Station. The izakaya lanes — as shutters fall, the lights under the tracks come on.
Best forFood-and-market people; repeat Tokyo visitors; anyone who prefers everyday local Tokyo.
ROUTE C

Akiba → Kanda → Jimbocho

Old Tokyo, page by page — a shrine, a brick viaduct, and a book town that goes quiet after dark.

FlowKanda Myojin → Manseibashi & mAAch ecute → Kanda station quarter → under-track lanes → Jimbocho book streets → the quiet book town
DistanceAbout 4 km · finish in Jimbocho
HighlightsManseibashi — the Chuo Line terminal of 1912, designed by Tatsuno Kingo, closed in 1943; its viaduct is now mAAch ecute. Old Kanda — one of Edo’s merchant and craftsman quarters. Jimbocho — around 130 secondhand bookshops gathered with the Meiji-era universities; browse while open, then see the shuttered, lamplit street.
Best forBook lovers and history lovers; anyone who prefers calm old Tokyo to the neon.

Practical notes

  • Meeting point — JR Akihabara Station, Electric Town exit area, at 16:00 for every route.
  • Finishing point — Akihabara (A), the Okachimachi–Ueno side (B), or Jimbocho (C), around 21:00. All are major stations.
  • Walking — roughly 3–4 km, mostly flat. Comfortable shoes, please; routes A and C include one flight of shrine steps.
  • Flexible — stops bend with the weather, the crowds and whatever catches your eye.
  • January evenings are cold — bring a warm layer; we are outside for much of the walk.
  • Closing times — small parts shops and market stalls wind down around 19:00–20:00; big stores, arcades and restaurants stay open late. Each route is sequenced around that.
  • Bring some cash — small stalls, market shops and shrine charms are often cash only.
  • Photos — your guide takes photos along the way and shares an album afterwards.
