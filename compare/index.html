Evening walk — 5 designs

The same page five ways: a proposal offering the guest three evening routes from Akihabara (夕方から夜) — A: Akihabara in Depth, B: to Okachimachi & Ameyoko, C: to Kanda & Jimbocho. No prices, no dates. Tell me the letter of the design you like.

A

House Standard

The familiar Yulia-style layout: cream, ink and red, photo tiles to jump to each route, then a card per route.

B

Night Neon

Dark navy with cyan and pink glow. Full-bleed night hero; each route is a glowing panel with a step-by-step line.

C

Editorial Magazine

A newspaper feel: serif display type on paper, drop cap, the three routes written up as magazine features.

D

Clean Minimal

White, one indigo accent, no decoration. Each route is a plain table: flow, distance, highlights, who it suits.

E

Bold Poster

Poster energy: huge condensed type, a giant 夕方 → 夜 graphic, and one big colour block per route.

All five variants carry the same content: three routes, the shared practical notes, and the guide’s own photographs. Historical notes were checked against the Japan Tourism Agency, the Akihabara Electrical Town Organization, Kanda Myojin and JR East histories. Nothing has been sent to Kioka or the guest — this board is for choosing the design.
